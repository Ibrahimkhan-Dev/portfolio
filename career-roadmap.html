Permanent Rule

The AI Development Workflow

Use AI as leverage. Never allow it to replace technical reasoning.

Step 1
Attempt
20–30 minutes of independent thinking before requesting AI assistance. Decompose the problem yourself first.
→
Step 2
AI
Use for hints, code review, debugging, explanations, alternative approaches. Ask: "Review my solution and give hints" — not "Write the whole thing."
→
Step 3
Understand
Before accepting generated code: what does it do, why does it work, what could fail, is there a simpler solution?
→
Step 4
Rewrite
For learning exercises: implement again from memory without copying. Preserve independent reasoning and debugging ability.
Weekly minimum: At least one 60–90 minute coding session with no AI tools — no Claude, no ChatGPT, no Copilot. The purpose is to preserve technical confidence, not to avoid AI permanently.
Guiding Principles

Twelve Career Principles

01 Protect income while transitioning. Family obligations come before career experiments.
02 Build fundamentals before tools. SQL and Python before Fabric and Spark.
03 Build projects before collecting certificates. Demonstrated capability beats credentials.
04 One cloud ecosystem, not many. Azure/Fabric deeply rather than broad and shallow.
05 Use AI heavily, but never surrender understanding. Leverage without dependency.
06 Measure progress by what you can build independently. Not by how many courses you finished.
07 Use the current job to create relevant experience. Zena Tech is a bridge, not a detour.
08 Talk to real engineers, not only instructors. Patterns across people beat one person's opinion.
09 Review the job market quarterly. Adjust 10–20% when evidence supports it. Do not restart.
10 Build transferable skills. No single job title should trap you in a dead end.
11 Relocate from strength, not desperation. Secure the opportunity first, then move.
12 Consistency beats intensity. 8–10 focused hours weekly beats unsustainable sprints followed by burnout.
